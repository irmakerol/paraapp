## Privacy Notice

This notice is given under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). It is information, not a consent form; where we need your explicit consent we ask for it on a separate screen.

### 1. Data controller

Fly Anatolia Hava Sporları Kulübü (association), Kordonboyu Mah. Ankara Cd. No:27 D:108, 34862 Kartal / İstanbul, Türkiye. Email: info@flyanatolia.com

### 2. Personal data we process

- **Identity and contact:** email address, display name, full name (optional), profile photo (optional).
- **Profile:** country/city, home site, pilot level, the year you started flying, wings used, your weight (optional, private unless you choose to show it), total flight hours and number of flights, short bio, licence number (optional, stored encrypted).
- **Age verification:** date of birth (to apply the minimum age and guardian consent for under-18s).
- **Guardian details (ages 14–17):** guardian's name and email, date of consent.
- **Account security:** session records, IP address and device information (processed by our authentication provider).
- **Consent and preference records:** which consent you gave or withdrew, when, and for which version of the text.
- **Community content:** news comments and likes, tips you write, gear ads and their photos, reports you file and people you block.
- **Messages:** one-to-one and group chat messages, photos and locations you choose to share in a chat.
- **Flights and location data:** the IGC/GPX files you import, GPS tracks, flight statistics, notes and the gear you used. Other people only ever see a track with the first and last 500 m removed, and only if the flight's visibility allows it; flights of users under 18 are always private.
- **Equipment:** your wings, harnesses and reserves with check and repack dates.
- **Flight scores:** a cross-country score (type, distance, points) worked out from your track; shown wherever the flight is visible.
- **Follows:** who you follow and who follows you.
- **Events and rides:** your answers to event invitations, and the carpool/retrieve posts you publish (adults only).
- **Trips:** the trips you follow, and when you sign up for a trip: your mobile phone, an emergency contact's name and phone, an optional note, the number of companions, a copy of your pilot level, years flying and flight count at sign-up, and the payment status the organisers record. The organisers and the trip's guides see these details.
- **Incident reports:** what happened, where and when, conditions and equipment. You choose whether the report is named, anonymous to the community or fully anonymous (then your account is not stored with it). **Injury details are health data** and are only stored with your explicit consent given in the report form.
- **Notifications:** push subscription details of your browser and your notification settings.

The SOS page shows your position only on your own device; it is not sent to us.

### 4. Recipients and transfers abroad

Data is stored on our service providers' servers in the European Union (Ireland and Germany). Transfers abroad rely on standard contractual clauses or other appropriate safeguards under KVKK Art. 9. We do not sell your data.

- **Within the app:** other users see what you choose to publish (profile fields per your privacy settings, ads, comments, public flights, ride posts). Moderators see a conversation only when it has been reported. Incident reports are seen only by the club's safety officers, never by other admins; every view of a report and of injury details is logged.
- **Service providers:** hosting and database (Supabase, EU), email delivery, and the browser push services of Google, Mozilla or Apple when you turn on notifications.
- **Only when you press a button:** map tiles from OpenFreeMap (and airspace tiles from OpenAIP, if shown) load when you press "Show map", and elevation tiles from AWS Open Data when you turn on the 3D replay; YouTube or Vimeo load when you play a video. These services then see your IP address. Weather is requested by our server with the site's coordinates only.
- **Sign in with Google**, if you choose it.

The current list of processors is available on request.

### 5. How we collect data

Electronically, through the forms in the app and when you sign in (email or Google).

### 6. Retention

When you delete your account your profile is anonymised immediately and the account is permanently deleted after 30 days; your files are deleted at once. Chats are kept until deleted. Carpool posts are deleted 30 days after the ride, event answers one year after the event. Trip contact details (phone and emergency contact) are deleted 30 days after the trip ends, and the rest of a trip registration after two years. In incident reports, the link to the reporter and any injury details are deleted one year after the report is closed; the anonymous report is kept for safety statistics. Other retention periods are available on request.

### 7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11, GDPR Arts. 15–22)

You can ask whether we process your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, take it with you (portability), and withdraw any consent. You can do most of this yourself on the app's "Privacy" page. For anything else, email info@flyanatolia.com; we answer within 30 days at the latest. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your EU supervisory authority.
