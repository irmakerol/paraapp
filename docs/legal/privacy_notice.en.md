## Privacy Notice

This notice is given under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). It is information, not a consent form; where we need your explicit consent we ask for it on a separate screen.

### 1. Data controller

Fly Anatolia Havacılık Kulübü (association), Kordonboyu Mah. Ankara Cd. No:27 D:108, 34862 Kartal / İstanbul, Türkiye. Email: info@flyanatolia.com

### 2. Personal data we process

- **Identity and contact:** email address, display name, full name (optional), profile photo (optional).
- **Profile:** country/city, home site, pilot level, years flying, wings used, short bio, licence number (optional, stored encrypted).
- **Age verification:** date of birth (to apply the minimum age and guardian consent for under-18s).
- **Guardian details (ages 14–17):** guardian's name and email, date of consent.
- **Account security:** session records, IP address and device information (processed by our authentication provider).
- **Consent and preference records:** which consent you gave or withdrew, when, and for which version of the text.

When flight logs (GPS tracks), ads, messages and incident reports are added in later versions, this notice will be updated and shown to you again.

### 4. Recipients and transfers abroad

Data is stored on our service providers' servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Transfers abroad rely on standard contractual clauses or other appropriate safeguards under KVKK Art. 9. The current list of processors is available in the app's data inventory and on request. We do not sell your data.

### 5. How we collect data

Electronically, through the forms in the app and when you sign in (email or Google).

### 6. Retention

When you delete your account your profile is anonymised immediately and the account is permanently deleted after 30 days. Other retention periods are listed in the data inventory.

### 7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11, GDPR Arts. 15–22)

You can ask whether we process your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, take it with you (portability), and withdraw any consent. You can do most of this yourself on the app's "Privacy" page. For anything else, email info@flyanatolia.com; we answer within 30 days at the latest. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your EU supervisory authority.
